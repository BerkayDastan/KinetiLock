2. Locally stored data

Ember Rush stores game progress and preferences locally on the device, including high scores, collected embers, unlocked cosmetic characters, sound, haptic, language and control preferences, tutorial status and gameplay statistics. These records are not associated with a user account and are not uploaded to our own game server.

3. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, advertising interactions, consent choices and diagnostic information.

Advertising consent in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland is managed through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising is not requested before the SDK confirms that ads may be requested. Users may accept, reject or manage their choices. The game remains playable if consent is refused or an advertisement is unavailable.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience but is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly create accounts for children or directly collect their personal information.

5. Data deletion

Local game progress can be deleted from the Settings screen by using the reset option, or by uninstalling the application. Advertising and consent preferences may also be managed through the privacy options available in the application.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its third-party services change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
