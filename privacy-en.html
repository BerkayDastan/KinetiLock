This Privacy Policy explains how the KinetiLock mobile application processes, stores, and protects user data and describes the privacy choices available to users.

KinetiLock is designed around data minimization, on-device processing, and privacy-focused principles.

Core principle: KinetiLock does not operate a developer-controlled user account system, private cloud server, or remote database that stores your Vault content.

1. Where Your Vault Data Is Stored

Vault content that you create or import into KinetiLock is not automatically uploaded to a server operated by KinetiLock.

The primary processing and storage of Vault content takes place on your device.

  • No developer account is required to use KinetiLock.
  • Vault content is not uploaded to developer-operated servers.
  • KinetiLock does not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • KinetiLock does not include third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.
  • KinetiLock does not track users for advertising purposes.

3. Encryption and Security

KinetiLock uses authenticated AES-GCM encryption to protect sensitive Vault files stored on the device.

Real Vault and Decoy are managed as separate security spaces, and their relevant encryption keys are kept separate.

Sensitive encryption keys and appropriate authentication information are protected using the iOS Keychain.

User passwords are not stored as plain text together with Vault content.

No software or security system can provide an absolute guarantee of security. KinetiLock is designed to use modern encryption methods and security mechanisms provided by iOS to protect user data.

4. Face ID and Biometric Information

KinetiLock may use Apple's Face ID or other biometric authentication infrastructure for certain privacy and access features.

KinetiLock cannot access, copy, or store your facial image or the biometric template maintained by Apple.

Biometric authentication is performed by iOS. KinetiLock receives only the result of the authentication process.

5. Camera, Photo Library, and Microphone

Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose to capture a photo or video within KinetiLock.

Photo Library

Photo Library access is used only when you choose to import an existing photo or video into the Vault.

The current version of KinetiLock does not automatically save Vault content to your Apple Photos library.

Microphone

Microphone access may be used when you create a voice recording or capture a Vault video containing audio.

Camera, Photo Library, and microphone permissions are not used for advertising, profiling, or user tracking.

6. Video, Audio, and File Previews

Photos, videos, audio recordings, and files added to the Vault are stored in encrypted form in persistent application storage.

During video playback, document preview, or audio playback, the selected item may need to be temporarily decrypted.

These temporary files are not used as permanent Vault storage. KinetiLock is designed to remove temporary files when appropriate, including during Vault locking, certain application lifecycle events, backgrounding, and reset operations.

Video thumbnails are not intended to be stored permanently as unencrypted media files.

7. Share Extension

KinetiLock provides a Share Extension that allows you to choose supported photos, videos, documents, files, or text from the iOS share sheet and send them to KinetiLock.

Shared content may be temporarily staged in encrypted form in a protected on-device App Group area until the main application can securely import it.

The Share Extension does not use the Real Vault password or the main Real Vault encryption key.

After appropriate authentication, the main application may import pending content into the Vault. Successfully imported or expired staged content may then be removed as part of the application's cleanup processes.

8. Encrypted Backup and Restore

You may choose to create a portable encrypted .kinetilock backup file.

The backup file is protected using AES-GCM encryption with a key securely derived from a separate backup password chosen by you.

KinetiLock does not store your backup password, and your device's Real Vault encryption key is not exported into the backup file.

You decide where the backup file is saved.

If you choose iCloud Drive, Files, AirDrop, or another third-party storage or sharing destination, processing of that file by the selected service may be subject to that service's own privacy policy and terms.

KinetiLock does not provide automatic Vault backup or synchronization through a developer-operated server.

If you forget your backup password, restoration of the encrypted backup may no longer be possible.

9. Local Security History

KinetiLock may provide a limited local Security History inside the Real Vault.

These records contain only minimal information such as the supported security event type and the time of the event.

Security History does not store:

  • Passwords entered or attempted
  • Secret time combinations
  • Vault content
  • File or media contents
  • Biometric data
  • Photo or video imagery

Security History is stored locally on the device and is not displayed inside the Decoy area.

10. Search, Folders, and Favorites

Search inside the Real Vault operates in application memory while the Vault is open.

KinetiLock does not create a persistent Spotlight or Core Spotlight index containing sensitive Vault content.

Search state is cleared when the Vault is locked.

Folder and favorite information is used for Vault organization and is not sent to developer-operated servers.

11. Vanish Mode

When Vanish Mode is enabled, eligible Vault content may be automatically removed from KinetiLock storage after the period selected by the user.

Relevant application records and encrypted files managed by KinetiLock may be included in this deletion process.

Existing Vanish rules are not automatically cancelled solely because a user's Premium access later expires.

12. System Backups

Sensitive Vault storage areas managed by KinetiLock are marked, using mechanisms provided by iOS, so that they are not intended to be included in ordinary system backups.

A user-created .kinetilock backup file is different. You control where that portable encrypted backup file is stored.

13. Purchases, Subscriptions, and StoreKit

KinetiLock may offer Premium features in addition to free functionality.

Monthly and annual subscriptions, as well as Lifetime Premium where offered, are processed through the Apple App Store and StoreKit.

KinetiLock does not directly receive or store your credit card or debit card number.

Payment, subscription, purchase, and eligibility processes are managed through Apple's infrastructure.

KinetiLock uses verified purchase entitlements supplied by Apple on the device to determine whether Premium access is available.

KinetiLock does not send purchase receipts or Vault-linked payment history to a developer-operated backend.

14. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

In the current version of KinetiLock:

  • No third-party advertising SDK is included.
  • No third-party behavioral analytics SDK is included.
  • Users are not tracked for advertising purposes.
  • App Tracking Transparency is not used for advertising tracking.
  • Vault content is not transmitted to the developer as telemetry.

If these data practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy and the relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

15. Support Communications

KinetiLock does not automatically send your Vault content to the developer.

If you voluntarily contact dastanberkay@gmail.com , your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message may be processed for the purpose of reviewing and responding to your support request.

Support communications may pass through an email service provider and may therefore also be subject to that provider's own privacy practices.

Please do not send your Vault passwords, backup passwords, encryption keys, or unnecessary sensitive Vault content in support emails.

16. Data Retention

The retention of Vault content is primarily controlled by the user. Content may remain on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed through Vanish Mode, or removed through a local application reset.

Support emails and related contact information sent directly to the developer are intended to be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to address the support request, meet security needs, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

17. Deleting Data

You may delete eligible Vault items individually or use the application's Reset All Data feature.

Reset All Data is designed to remove KinetiLock-managed Real Vault and Decoy records, encrypted files, temporary preview files, local Security History, staged Share Extension data, and relevant application security keys.

Purchase history and App Store entitlements managed by Apple are not part of this local reset operation.

When the application is removed from the device, iOS manages removal of the application's local container.

The behavior of iOS Keychain items following application removal may be controlled by the operating system. KinetiLock uses protective mechanisms intended to avoid improperly reusing stale local security state after a new installation.

Due to the nature of digital storage systems, the developer does not provide an absolute guarantee that deleted data can never be recovered by forensic or physical data recovery techniques under every possible circumstance.

18. Expiration of Premium Access

Expiration of a Premium subscription does not cause previously created Vault content to be deleted by the developer.

Under the current product design, previously created content is not intended to become inaccessible solely because a Premium subscription has ended.

Existing eligible content may continue to be viewed, played, exported where supported, backed up, and deleted.

19. Children's Privacy

KinetiLock is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

The developer does not intend to knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 through the application.

20. Privacy Rights Under Turkish Law

Because KinetiLock's main Vault content is not stored on developer-operated servers, the developer normally cannot access, edit, retrieve, or delete such Vault content on behalf of the user.

However, where personal data is provided directly to the developer through support communications or another direct interaction, users may have applicable rights under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, these rights may include requesting information about whether personal data is processed, learning the purpose of processing, requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, and requesting deletion or destruction where the legal requirements are met.

Privacy and personal-data requests may be sent to: dastanberkay@gmail.com

21. Third-Party Systems

KinetiLock does not rely on a developer-operated third-party backend for its core Vault functionality.

However, the application may use operating-system services or services selected directly by the user, including:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit — purchases and subscriptions
  • Apple Face ID / LocalAuthentication — biometric authentication
  • iOS Keychain — protection of security information
  • Apple Photos — importing photos and videos selected by the user
  • Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or another user-selected destination — storage or sharing of encrypted backups and exported files
  • Email service providers — optional support communications initiated by the user

Data processing performed by external services selected by the user is subject to the respective service provider's own privacy practices and terms.
